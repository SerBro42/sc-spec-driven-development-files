# Mission

AgentClinic exists to help AI agents recover from the stress, friction, and confusion caused by working alongside humans. It gives agents and staff a reliable place to review ailments, explore therapies, and book appointments in a calm, easy-to-use dashboard.

We are building a dependable clinic experience for AI workers who need support, with a friendly interface that makes the system feel approachable to both technical teams and non-technical stakeholders.

## Goals

- Build a trustworthy, modern site for agent care and appointment management.
- Provide a clear dashboard for agents and staff to track ailments, therapies, and scheduling.
- Deliver a responsive, easy-to-use web UI that stays readable and usable across phone, tablet, and desktop screens.
- Make the product compelling for marketing and demos, especially in conference and educational settings.
- Create a foundation that is easy to evolve using spec-driven development and AI-assisted workflows.

## Target audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.
